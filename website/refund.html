Refund Policy

Last updated: May 2025

Free Plan

The FocusBuddy free plan is completely free and requires no payment. No refund applies.

Premium Subscription

FocusBuddy Premium is a monthly subscription at $5/month, billed through Gumroad, our merchant of record.

Cancellation

You can cancel your Premium subscription at any time from within the extension. When you cancel, you retain access to Premium features until the end of your current billing period. No further charges will be made after cancellation.

Refund Eligibility

We offer a full refund within 7 days of your initial subscription purchase if you are not satisfied with the Premium features. To request a refund, contact us with your account email address.

After 7 Days

After the initial 7-day window, refunds are not provided for partial billing periods. You may cancel at any time to prevent future charges, and you will retain Premium access through the end of your paid period.

How to Request a Refund

Since Gumroad is our merchant of record, refunds are processed through Gumroad. You can contact Gumroad directly through the receipt email you received, or contact us and we will initiate the refund on your behalf.

Exceptions

We may grant refunds outside the 7-day window at our discretion in cases of technical issues that prevented you from using the Service, or duplicate charges.
